## Full description — English
Become the Khan of the steppe and grow your nomad camp into the Great Mongol Empire!

🏕 BUILD YOUR CAMP — Place gers, pens, workshops and farms. Every villager has a home and a job: herders, woodcutters, hunters, builders. Day and night cycles, four changing seasons, lamps that glow at night.

🐎 RAISE LIVESTOCK — Horses, cattle, camels, sheep and goats. Produce milk, meat, wool, felt, buuz, airag and aaruul. Each season changes the rules: winter brings less yield and more wolves.

⚔ COLLECT HEROES — Summon historical heroes such as Genghis Khan, Subutai, Khasar, Börte and Muqali, upgrade their stars and equip them.

🛡 BATTLE AND TACTICS — Fight through the historical campaign chapter by chapter, choose your troops and defend your herds from raiders.

🌍 WORLD AND CLANS — Campaign across a 13th-century world map. 10 clans, clan wars, friends, chat and leaderboards.

🏹 NAADAM — Compete in archery, horse racing and wrestling and win trophies.

Light and fast, made for phones. Registration is optional; back up your progress to the cloud and continue on another device.
